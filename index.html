About

Scholarship with purpose.

I am an interdisciplinary academic and researcher working at the intersection of operations, quality management, engineering systems and sustainability.

My work connects rigorous research methods with practical organizational challenges—from Industry 4.0 adoption and continuous improvement to circular economy transitions and resilient supply chains. I bring together experience in higher education, doctoral research, programme leadership and industry-facing projects.
